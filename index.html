About Us

Locally owned and operated in Sherman, Texas

Lopez Lawn & Landscaping is a locally owned lawn care and landscaping company proudly serving Sherman and the surrounding community. We show up, get the job done, and take pride in making outdoor spaces look their best.

Whether you need a small touch-up or a full outdoor makeover, we treat your property like it's our own — with quality work, honest prices, and dependable service every time. We are grateful for every customer who trusts us with their home, leaves a review, and helps this small business grow.
