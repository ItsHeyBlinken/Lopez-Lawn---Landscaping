# Product Context

This demo is a pitch tool, not a live production site. The audience is the business owner. Success looks like a page that feels like their company, uses their voice, and shows how customers would request a quote.

## Customer path

1. See local branding and recent work
2. Scan services they actually offer
3. Call, text, or email for a free quote

## Product constraints

- No backend or database
- No invented testimonials, years in business, or certifications
- Prefer call/text because that is how they currently book work
